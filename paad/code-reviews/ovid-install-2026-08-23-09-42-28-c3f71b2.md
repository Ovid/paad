## Executive Summary

The `preview/` tree is structurally sound — the two trees differ only in the three documented markers, `make test` is green, and no untrusted-data preamble or read-only tool grant was weakened anywhere in the shipped material. The two Critical findings are both about the branch's relationship to its own stated goal: `agentic-review`'s rewritten pre-flight digraph routes the **zero-argument default invocation** into a STOP node, and `plugins/` was hand-edited on this branch at an already-released version, so merging reopens the exact leak the branch's own documentation declares closed. Beneath those, a consistent theme: `make release` now performs its largest, most destructive mutation — `rsync -a --delete` over the shipped tree — *before* the checks that can refuse it, and several documents (README above all) still describe the one-tree world the branch replaced. Confidence is high; twelve specialists ran, four of the top findings were independently corroborated by four lenses each, and the highest-severity mechanical claims were reproduced by execution in a scratch copy.
